    Supermarket & Supervillain Operator Manual

    1. Opening the Menu

    Press [TAB] or walk into the office (top left) and interact [E/SPACE] with the computer to open the menu.

    2. Stock & Pricing Tab

    Set your prices and target stock levels here before opening. You may want to raise prices or lower prices based on tomorrow's demand. The bulk button orders stock of an item. Shelf stock is the current item count on the aisles; target is the maximum.

    3. Layout & Staff Tab

    Upgrade aisle shelving tiers, hire staff, and boost marketing campaigns to increase the number of shoppers. A stocker will automatically stock items for you. Set stocker controls to choose how much product they will order for the next day.

    4. Office & Automation Tab

    View revenue stats, open and close shop, and automate your workflow.

    5. Tutorial & Guide

    You can come here to view the tutorial at any time.

    6. Admin Mode & Menu Closing

    You can unlock Admin Mode with the passcode. Close the menu using [E/SPACE] near the computer, [TAB], or by clicking the red X in the top right

    7. Stocking the Floor

    The aisles are empty right now. Open up the menu and order stock of an item by clicking Bulk. Walk over to the door in the bottom right of the store to enter the storage room where you can grab items to put on the aisles. Hover over a shelf with your mouse to see what is on it. Walk up to a shelf and grab some items. You can only hold 5 items at a time. Leave the storage room. Now walk up to an empty aisle and press [E] or [SPACE] to restock items. Each aisle can hold 10 items. Make sure to restock to prevent walkouts. Once you finish stocking, open the menu again [TAB], go to Office & Automation, and click 'Start Next Day Shift' to open the shop

    8. Talking to Customers

    Walk up to any shopper in the store and press [E] or [SPACE] to chat with them and hear their stories

    9. Checking out Customers

    Hire a cashier or stand at the register (bottom left) and press [E] to check out waiting shoppers when they reach the front. Closing before all shoppers have been served is considered closing early; closing early may result in loss of job.

    10. Closing Store & EOD Report

    When the day is done, open the menu [TAB], go to Office & Automation, and click 'Close Shop & End Day Shift'. Review your financial audit, net daily profits, and customer satisfaction ratings in the end-of-day report.

    11. Entering & Leaving the Lair

    Move back and forth between your secret lair and the front store anytime by interacting [E/SPACE] with the bookcase.

    12. Jarvis the Butler

    Walk up to Butler Jarvis and press [E] or [SPACE] to receive secret underworld intel and syndicate status. Then interact with the Supercomputer terminal (blinking) on the right.

    13. Supercomputer Operations

    Do supervillain missions (can be done after the shop is closed), and look at the active heroes and supervillain leaderboard. Click Close Feed in the top right. • Drone Missions & Orbital Death-Ray: Unlocked at Computer Level 4. Target districts with the satellite laser!
    • Hero Attributes & Damage: Heroes possess attributes like Flight, Invulnerability, Speed, Telekinesis, etc. Omega heroes block all casualties.
    • Escalation Thresholds: Casualties $\ge 1$ triggers vigilante hunters. Casualties $\ge 25$ unlocks Supervillain Status with extreme hero retaliation and syndicate threats!

    14. Existing Menu Tab Changes

    In Office & Automation you can siphon funds to use for villainy. In Tutorial & Guide the villain tutorial will also now show up here for reference for the rest of the game.

    15. New Menu Tabs

    In villain Leaderboard to view the villain leaderboard and look at the active heroes. See heroes patrol sectors, nemesis' and threat level. Click on a hero to see their superpowers. Supervillains are villains who have 25+ casualties. In Lair Upgrades upgrade your supercomputer, deploy a robotic doppelganger to run the store, and synthesize mutants. In Inbox see any messages you receive.
